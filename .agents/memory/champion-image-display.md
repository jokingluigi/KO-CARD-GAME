---
name: Champion image display settings
description: The product decision for how Champion portrait presentation settings apply across portrait variants.
---

Champion basic and quest-complete portraits use one shared display configuration for mode, scale, and X/Y position. The active portrait changes after quest completion, but its framing stays consistent.

**Why:** The controls describe the Champion portrait presentation rather than a particular uploaded asset, and separate settings would make the same Champion shift framing unexpectedly during a match.

**How to apply:** Keep the settings on the Champion record and pass them to every Champion artwork surface, including the active quest-complete portrait.
